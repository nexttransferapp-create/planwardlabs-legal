Next Transfer Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

Developer: Planward Labs

Privacy & Support: next.transfer.app@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Next Transfer is a productivity and planning application designed to help returned missionaries organize tasks, events, goals, contacts, plans, reminders, locations, and related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Next Transfer accesses, uses, stores, and handles information in the current version of the application.

2. Local-First Design

Next Transfer is currently designed as a local-first application.

Planner information such as tasks, calendar events, goals, weekly plans, contacts, notes, preferences, reminder settings, and saved locations is stored primarily in private application storage on your device.

The current version of Next Transfer does not require an online user account, does not provide Planward Labs cloud synchronization, and does not upload the local planner database to a Planward Labs-operated cloud database.

3. Information You Provide

You may choose to enter information into Next Transfer, including:

  • tasks and planning information
  • calendar events
  • goals and activities
  • weekly plans
  • notes
  • contact information
  • an optional local profile or display name
  • saved map locations or coordinates
  • reminder and notification preferences
  • application appearance and other preferences

This information is stored locally in the application's private database or application storage on your device.

The current version does not upload this planner database to a Planward Labs server.

4. Device Contacts

Next Transfer includes an optional feature that allows you to import contacts from your device.

If you choose to use this feature, Next Transfer may request permission to read your device contacts.

The import feature may access contact information needed for the import process, including:

  • contact name
  • phone number
  • email address

The import process is initiated by you, and you choose which contacts to import.

Imported contact information is stored locally inside Next Transfer.

Next Transfer does not modify or write to your device's original contacts.

You may continue using manual contact and planning features without granting contact access.

5. Location Access

Next Transfer includes an optional Locate me feature for Maps.

If you use this feature, Next Transfer may request Android permission for approximate and/or precise location. Android may allow you to grant only approximate location even when precise location is requested.

Location access is requested contextually when you use location-dependent functionality. Next Transfer does not require location permission merely to display the base map.

Next Transfer does not request background location permission and does not continuously track your location in the background.

When you use Locate me, your current device location is used to perform the requested map action, such as recentering the map. Your current location is not automatically turned into a saved Next Transfer location unless you separately choose to save or associate a location through an available app feature.

6. Google Maps

Next Transfer uses the Google Maps SDK for Android and Google Maps Platform to display map content.

When Google Maps content is displayed, your device communicates with Google to obtain and render map content and related services.

According to Google's current Maps SDK for Android disclosures, the SDK may automatically collect or process technical information such as request and device metadata, IP address, SDK-specific identifiers, and crash or stability information. Depending on how map functionality is used, Google may also process map interaction data such as panning or zooming.

Planward Labs does not control Google's independent collection, processing, retention, or use of information handled by Google Maps Platform.

Google Maps Platform and related Google services are governed by Google's terms and privacy practices. Please review the Google Privacy Policy and applicable Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms.

7. Saved Locations and Map Pins

Next Transfer may allow you to place or save map pins associated with supported app records.

Coordinates that you intentionally save or associate with a record are stored locally in Next Transfer.

A manually saved location may come from a point you selected on the map or, where supported, from a location-related action you explicitly initiated.

If you explicitly choose to open a location in an external navigation application, the relevant coordinates or location information may be passed to that external application through the Android operating system.

8. External Applications

Certain Next Transfer features may allow you to open other applications installed on your device.

For example, where supported, you may choose to:

  • call a contact
  • open an SMS application
  • compose an email
  • open WhatsApp or another supported application
  • open a navigation application

These actions occur only when you initiate them.

Information needed for the requested action may be passed to the external application you choose.

Next Transfer does not read your conversations and does not automatically record what happens after you leave Next Transfer.

The privacy practices of external applications are governed by their respective privacy policies.

9. Privacy Lock and Device Authentication

Next Transfer offers an optional Privacy Lock feature that may use authentication provided by your device, including biometric authentication or device credentials.

Authentication is performed by the device operating system.

Next Transfer does not receive or store your fingerprint, facial recognition template, or other biometric template.

10. Notifications and Reminders

Next Transfer can schedule local notifications and reminders for supported planning features, such as events, tasks, goals, reviews, reports, or follow-up activities.

On supported Android versions, Next Transfer may request notification permission before displaying notifications.

Notification scheduling and delivery are handled locally through the application and Android system services. The current version does not use a Planward Labs remote push-notification server to deliver these reminders.

Next Transfer may use Android background scheduling and boot-completion capabilities to maintain or restore eligible local reminder schedules after device restarts.

Depending on your Next Transfer privacy settings and Android lock-screen notification settings, a notification may display information derived from planner records outside the unlocked app. Privacy-oriented or generic notification presentation may be used to reduce contextual details where supported.

You can control notification permission through Android settings and supported reminder options inside Next Transfer.

11. App Permissions and Device Capabilities

The current Android version of Next Transfer may use the following permissions or platform capabilities for the stated purposes:

  • Internet: to load Google Maps content and communicate with supported third-party services when you use connected functionality.
  • Contacts: to import device contacts when you choose to use the import feature.
  • Approximate and precise location: for user-initiated location functionality such as Locate me. No background location permission is requested.
  • Notifications: to display local reminders and notifications where Android requires permission.
  • Biometric/device authentication: for the optional Privacy Lock feature.
  • Boot completion/background scheduling: to support eligible local reminder and background scheduling behavior after device restarts.

Permission-dependent features are designed to be requested in context where practical. Denying an optional permission may disable only the related feature while leaving other parts of Next Transfer available.

12. Analytics, Advertising, Tracking, and Diagnostics

Next Transfer does not contain advertising, does not sell advertising space, and does not use an advertising ID for targeted advertising.

Planward Labs does not currently operate a behavioral advertising or data-broker business based on information stored in Next Transfer.

The current app does not include a Planward Labs-operated behavioral analytics service or a Planward Labs-operated remote crash-reporting service.

However, third-party platform components used by the app may collect technical diagnostics or usage information according to their own policies. In particular, Google states that the Maps SDK for Android may collect request/device metadata, IP address, SDK identifiers, crash or stability data, and certain map interaction events. See Section 6.

13. Accounts, Cloud Sync, and Backup

The current version of Next Transfer does not require or provide an online user account.

Planward Labs cloud backup and synchronization are not currently active.

Android application backup is disabled in the current Next Transfer application configuration.

Next Transfer may introduce optional account, export, backup, restore, synchronization, or other connected functionality in future versions. If such functionality changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures will be updated before or when that functionality becomes available.

14. Data Storage and Security

Next Transfer stores application information primarily in private application storage on the user's device.

The current Next Transfer local database should not be considered fully encrypted or end-to-end encrypted.

Next Transfer may use Android platform security protections and the optional Privacy Lock feature to provide additional protection against unauthorized access.

Android application backup is disabled for the current Next Transfer application configuration.

No method of electronic storage or device security can guarantee absolute security.

15. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current version of Next Transfer primarily stores information locally, information generally remains on the device according to the lifecycle of the applicable record.

Depending on the type of information, records may be:

  • deleted
  • archived
  • cancelled
  • merged
  • retained locally for historical functionality

Users can remove or modify information using the controls available for each supported feature.

Local reminder schedules associated with records may also be changed or removed as the related record or reminder settings change.

Uninstalling Next Transfer normally removes the application's private local database and application data from the device.

The current version does not maintain a Planward Labs-hosted cloud copy of the user's planner database.

16. Children's Privacy

Next Transfer is designed as a productivity and planning application for returned missionaries and is not specifically directed toward children.

The current application does not contain advertising or public social-network features.

17. Third-Party Services

The current version of Next Transfer may interact with third-party software, services, or applications, including:

  • Google Maps Platform / Google Maps SDK for Android
  • Android operating-system services used for permissions, notifications, background scheduling, and device authentication
  • external applications explicitly opened by the user

Third parties may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Next Transfer's use of a third-party service does not mean that Planward Labs controls that third party's independent privacy practices.

18. No Sale of Personal Information

Planward Labs does not sell personal information collected through the current version of Next Transfer.

The current version also does not operate an advertising or data-broker business based on information stored in Next Transfer.

19. Future Features

Next Transfer is under active development.

Future versions may introduce functionality such as online accounts, export, cloud backup, synchronization, optional analytics, remote crash reporting, or other connected services.

Such functionality must not be interpreted as active merely because it is mentioned here.

When a future feature materially changes Next Transfer's data practices, Planward Labs intends to update this Privacy Policy and applicable Google Play disclosures to reflect the functionality actually shipped.

20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Next Transfer's features, technologies, services, or legal requirements change.

When material changes are made, the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
