1. Introduction

Next Transfer is a productivity and planning application designed to help returned missionaries organize tasks, calendar events, goals, weekly plans, contacts, reminders, locations, and related information.

This Privacy Policy explains what Next Transfer accesses, what it stores, where that information is kept, what leaves your device, and what you can do about it. It describes the version of Next Transfer identified above. When the application's behavior changes, this policy is updated to match what actually ships.

2. Summary

  • Next Transfer is a local-first application. Your planner database lives in private application storage on your own device.
  • Next Transfer requires no online account, and Planward Labs does not operate a cloud copy of your planner database.
  • Next Transfer contains no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting service.
  • Planward Labs does not sell your information.
  • Next Transfer can request optional access to contacts, location, and notifications for specific features you choose to use. Each is requested only when the related feature is used.
  • Backup & Restore lets you create a copy of your Next Transfer data and save it to a location you choose. Next Transfer does not automatically upload backup files to Planward Labs. Portable backup files are not encrypted — see Section 7.

3. Information You Enter

Depending on which features you use, you may enter or create the following information inside Next Transfer:

  • an optional local profile or display name
  • tasks, including due dates, times, priorities, status, and completion history
  • calendar events, including repeating-event and exception data
  • goals, goal activities, achievement history, and progress entries
  • weekly plans and planning targets
  • outcome reports and activity history
  • contacts, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, groups, tags, and availability
  • saved places and map coordinates
  • reminder and notification preferences
  • privacy and appearance preferences
  • application settings and first-run progress

Where it is stored: in a private SQLite database and private application storage belonging to Next Transfer on your device. This information is not sent to a Planward Labs server, because the current version of Next Transfer has no Planward Labs server component.

4. Information Next Transfer Accesses From Your Device

4.1 Contacts

Next Transfer includes an optional feature for importing contacts from your device's contact list.

  • The feature is initiated by you, and you choose which contacts to import.
  • Next Transfer asks Android for contact access only when you open that feature.
  • Next Transfer reads only contact names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
  • Next Transfer does not modify, add to, or delete your device's contacts.
  • Imported contact information is stored locally inside Next Transfer.
  • You can use manual contact entry and all other planning features without granting contact access.

4.2 Location

Next Transfer includes an optional Locate me action for Maps.

  • Location access is requested only after you explicitly use a location-dependent action such as Locate me. It is not required merely to display a map.
  • Android may allow you to grant approximate location only, even when precise location is requested.
  • Next Transfer does not request background location permission and does not track your location in the background.
  • Your current location is used to perform the action you asked for. It is not automatically converted into a saved location unless you separately choose to save it.
  • Coordinates you deliberately save or associate with a record are stored locally inside Next Transfer.

5. Device Permissions

The current Android version of Next Transfer declares seven permissions, each tied to a specific feature:

  • Internet: load Google Maps map content and reach connected functionality when you use it.
  • Contacts: import device contacts when you choose to use the import feature.
  • Approximate and precise location: foreground location for user-initiated actions such as Locate me. No background location.
  • Notifications: display local reminders and notifications on Android versions that require permission.
  • Biometric/device authentication: support the optional Privacy Lock feature through your device's own authentication.
  • Boot completion: restore eligible local reminder schedules after your device restarts or the clock or time zone changes.

Next Transfer does not request broad storage access. Saving a backup on supported Android versions happens without any storage permission (see Section 7).

Permission-dependent features are designed to fail softly: declining an optional permission disables the related feature and leaves the rest of Next Transfer usable.

6. Notifications and Reminders

  • Next Transfer schedules local reminders for supported features such as events, tasks, goals, reviews, and follow-up activities.
  • Reminders are scheduled and delivered by the application and Android system services on your device. The current version does not use a remote push-notification server.
  • Next Transfer may use Android background scheduling and boot-completion capability to keep eligible reminder schedules working after a restart.
  • Depending on your Next Transfer privacy settings and your Android lock-screen settings, a notification may show information derived from your planner records. Next Transfer provides a privacy setting to hide notification content.
  • You can control notification permission through Android settings and adjust reminder preferences inside Next Transfer.
  • In-app update messages shown inside Next Transfer (for example, release notes for a new beta version) are ordinary application screens. They do not require notification permission and do not produce system notifications.

7. Backup & Restore

Backup & Restore is a user-initiated feature introduced in the current beta. It is designed so that you keep control of your own data.

What a backup contains. A backup contains the persistent Next Transfer data for your profile, including your planner records (tasks, calendar events, goals, weekly plans), outcome reports and activity history, contacts and contact details, saved places, and your preferences (including reminder, notification, privacy, and appearance settings). Device-specific items that cannot meaningfully be restored on another device are excluded: Android permission grants, notification scheduling artifacts, and first-run progress.

Where a backup is saved. On supported Android versions, Next Transfer writes the backup directly to your device's user-visible Downloads folder using Android's MediaStore interface. This requires no storage permission and does not give Next Transfer access to other files on your device. If your Android version cannot write there without a broad storage permission, Next Transfer instead asks you to choose a destination using Android's system file picker.

What this means about storage providers. The destination you choose may be a cloud-backed provider installed on your device, such as Google Drive. If you choose such a provider, that provider stores and processes the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Next Transfer does not automatically upload backup files to our servers. When you choose a location for a backup, the selected storage provider may store or process that file according to its own terms and privacy policy.

Encryption. Portable Next Transfer backup files (.ntbackup) are not encrypted. Anyone who gains access to a backup file may be able to read the information it contains. Backup files include an integrity hash so that a damaged or altered file is rejected, but an integrity hash is not encryption. Store backup files somewhere you consider secure, and treat them as you would treat the application itself.

Separate recovery checkpoint. Before applying a restore, Next Transfer creates a temporary recovery checkpoint of your current local data. That checkpoint is stored in Next Transfer's private application storage, is protected with AES-256-GCM using a key held in Android's secure storage on that device, and is never written to a user document, exported, or uploaded. It exists only so a restore can be undone locally.

Restoring. Restore reads a .ntbackup file that you select, validates it, and then applies it in one of two ways: Merge (combine the backup with your current local data) or Replace (replace the current local data with the backup). A restore always takes place after a verified recovery checkpoint has been created.

Your control. You may delete backup files at any time using your device's file manager or your chosen storage provider. You may also remove Next Transfer's local recovery data from inside the application.

8. Android System Backup

Next Transfer disables Android's automatic application backup. Your Next Transfer data is therefore not copied into Android's automatic cloud backup or device-to-device transfer. The only way a copy of your data leaves the application's private storage is when you personally create a backup (Section 7) or export readable data.

9. Data Transmission and Cloud Behavior

  • The current version of Next Transfer has no user account, no sign-in, no Planward Labs cloud synchronization, and no Planward Labs-operated database containing your planner data.
  • Next Transfer does not itself transmit your planner records, contacts, notes, or saved locations to Planward Labs.
  • The application does communicate over the network when you use connected functionality — principally Google Maps map rendering (Section 10) — and when you personally send a file or message to an external application or storage provider.
  • Personal and sensitive data that is transmitted is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) by the platform services involved.

10. Third-Party Services

Google Maps. Next Transfer uses the Google Maps SDK for Android and Google Maps Platform to display map content. When map content is displayed, your device communicates with Google to obtain and render it. Google states that the Maps SDK for Android may collect or process technical information such as request and device metadata, IP address, SDK-specific identifiers, and crash or stability information, and, depending on how map functionality is used, map interaction data such as panning or zooming. Planward Labs does not control Google's independent collection, processing, retention, or use of that information. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms.

Android platform services. Android provides the operating-system services Next Transfer relies on for permissions, notification delivery, background scheduling, secure storage, file selection, and device authentication.

External applications you open. Where supported, you can choose to call a contact, open an SMS application, compose an email, open a messaging application such as WhatsApp, or open a navigation application. These actions occur only when you initiate them, and the information needed for the action is passed to the application you chose. Next Transfer does not read your conversations and does not record what happens after you leave the application. Those applications' privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Storage providers you choose. If you save a backup to a cloud-backed destination, that provider's terms and privacy policy apply to the file.

11. Analytics, Advertising, and Crash Reporting

  • Next Transfer contains no advertising and no advertising SDK, and does not use an advertising identifier for targeted advertising.
  • Next Transfer contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. Planward Labs does not operate a behavioral analytics or remote crash-reporting service for this application.
  • Planward Labs does not sell personal information, and does not operate a data-broker business based on information stored in Next Transfer.
  • The application includes a local diagnostic facility that can produce a preview you can review on your device before choosing to share it. It is not transmitted automatically. Third-party platform components described in Section 10 may still collect technical diagnostics under their own policies.

12. Privacy Lock and Device Authentication

Next Transfer offers an optional Privacy Lock that can use authentication provided by your device, including biometric authentication or your device credentials. Authentication is performed by the operating system. Next Transfer does not receive or store your fingerprint, face template, PIN, or other authentication secret.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Next Transfer stores information locally, your information generally remains on your device according to the lifecycle of each record. Depending on the type, records may be edited, completed, archived, cancelled, merged, or deleted using the controls available in each feature. Reminder schedules associated with a record are updated or removed when the record or your reminder settings change.

You can remove your data by:

  • deleting individual records inside the application,
  • clearing application data from Android settings, or
  • uninstalling Next Transfer, which normally removes its private database and application data from the device.

Files you created and saved yourself — for example a .ntbackup file in Downloads or in a cloud provider — are not removed by uninstalling the application. You control those files and should delete them through your device's file manager or your storage provider if you no longer want them.

The current version has no account and therefore no server-side account data to delete. The Planward Labs account-deletion page applies to products that offer accounts.

14. Security

Next Transfer stores information in private application storage protected by Android's application sandbox and, on supported devices, by device encryption. The optional Privacy Lock adds a further barrier against casual access to the application. The temporary recovery checkpoint is encrypted as described in Section 7.

This policy does not claim that the Next Transfer database is fully encrypted or end-to-end encrypted, and it does not claim that backups are encrypted. Portable backup files are expressly not encrypted. No method of electronic storage or device security can guarantee absolute security.

15. Children's Privacy

Next Transfer is designed as a productivity and planning application for returned missionaries and is not directed toward children. It contains no advertising and no public social-network features. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to Next Transfer, contact us at the address in Section 18.

16. Your Choices

  • Use Next Transfer entirely without granting contacts or location access.
  • Decline or revoke any Android permission at any time in Android settings; the related feature stops working and the rest of the application continues to work.
  • Hide notification content using the privacy setting inside Next Transfer.
  • Export or delete your data yourself, including removing backups you created.
  • Send a backup file to another device or storage location of your choosing.
