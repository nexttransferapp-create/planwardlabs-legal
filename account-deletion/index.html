Planward Labs Next Transfer Delete your Next Transfer account This page explains how to delete your Next Transfer account (developer: Planward Labs) and the data stored with it. You only have an account if you signed in with Google. If you never signed in, nothing is stored on our servers. Your data is only on your phone. Option 1: In the app (immediate) Open Next Transfer and sign in with the account you want to delete. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account & cloud data. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted right away. This cannot be undone. Option 2: By email (if you no longer have the app) Email next.transfer.app@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with. Use the subject Delete my Next Transfer account. We complete email requests within 30 days and confirm by email. What is deleted Your Next Transfer account (sign-in record and email address). All your Google Sync cloud copies and their sync details. Your Pro record (plan, status and the hashed purchase reference). If you were a beta tester, your email stays on the beta list until the launch offer ends and the list is deleted. To have it removed sooner, email us. What is not deleted Data on your phone. To remove it, uninstall Next Transfer or clear its storage in Android settings. Backup files you saved (for example a .ntbackup file in Downloads or Google Drive). Delete them with your file manager or storage provider. Your Pro subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
